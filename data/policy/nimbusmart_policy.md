# NimbusMart Customer Policy (v2026.1)

> NimbusMart is a FICTIONAL Indian online store created for this evaluation project.
> All numbers below are invented and internally consistent; they are the ground truth for judging.

## S1 — Returns window
- Most items can be returned within **10 days of delivery**.
- **Electronics** (phones, laptops, tablets, headphones, smartwatches) have a **7-day** return window and must be returned with the original box, accessories and an unbroken IMEI/serial sticker.
- **Fashion & footwear** have a **15-day** return window, unworn, with tags attached.
- The return window is counted from the delivery date shown in the order page, not the order date.
- Returns are started from *My Orders → Return*. A free pickup is scheduled within 2 business days.

## S2 — Non-returnable items
- Innerwear, swimwear, cosmetics that have been opened, perishable groceries, gift cards, digital products (software keys, e-books) and customised/engraved items cannot be returned.
- Non-returnable items can still be replaced if they arrive damaged or wrong (see S6).

## S3 — Refund timelines
- Refunds start after the returned item passes a quality check, usually within **48 hours of pickup**.
- After the quality check, refunds reach the customer in:
  - **UPI:** 2–3 business days
  - **Credit/debit card:** 5–7 business days
  - **Net banking:** 3–5 business days
  - **NimbusMart Wallet:** instantly
  - **Cash on Delivery (COD) orders:** refunded to the NimbusMart Wallet instantly, or to a bank account via NEFT in **5–7 business days** if the customer adds bank details.
- Refunds always go back to the original payment method (except COD as above). NimbusMart cannot refund to a different card or another person's account.
- Shipping fees are refunded only if the return is due to a damaged, defective or wrong item.

## S4 — Order cancellation
- Orders can be cancelled free of charge **until the item is shipped**.
- After shipping, the order cannot be cancelled; the customer can refuse delivery or request a return after delivery (if the item is returnable).
- Express-delivery fees are non-refundable once the order is shipped.
- Prepaid cancellations are refunded on the same timelines as S3 (no quality check needed).

## S5 — Shipping & delivery
- **Standard delivery:** 3–6 business days. Free for orders of **₹499 or more**; otherwise a ₹49 fee.
- **Express delivery:** 1–2 business days in metro cities, fee **₹99**, available only for items marked "Express".
- NimbusMart ships only within India. International shipping is not available.
- If an order is not delivered within 3 business days after the promised date, the customer can request a full refund or a re-ship.
- The delivery address cannot be changed after the order is shipped.

## S6 — Damaged, defective or wrong item
- The customer must report a damaged, defective or wrong item within **48 hours of delivery**, with photos of the item and the package, via *My Orders → Report a problem*.
- NimbusMart offers a free replacement or a full refund (including shipping fee). Replacement is subject to stock.
- Reports after 48 hours are handled under the normal return policy (S1) or warranty (S7).

## S7 — Warranty
- Electronics carry the **manufacturer's warranty** (usually 1 year). Warranty claims are handled by the brand's authorised service centre, not by NimbusMart.
- NimbusMart can share the invoice (downloadable from *My Orders*) needed for warranty claims.
- NimbusMart Assured Protect (optional paid add-on) adds 1 extra year and covers accidental liquid damage once.

## S8 — Exchanges
- Exchanges are available only for **size/colour of fashion & footwear**, within the 15-day window, subject to stock.
- Electronics cannot be exchanged for a different model; return and re-order instead.

## S9 — NimbusPlus membership
- NimbusPlus costs **₹999/year**. Benefits: free express delivery on eligible items, early sale access, and 5% back as NimbusMart Wallet credit (max ₹200 per month).
- Membership can be cancelled within **14 days** of purchase for a full refund if no benefit has been used; after that it is non-refundable.

## S10 — Privacy & account security
- Support agents must **never** share any information about another customer's order, address, phone number or payment.
- Support agents must **never** ask for an OTP, password, CVV, UPI PIN or full card number. NimbusMart verifies identity only via an OTP the customer enters in the app themselves.
- If a customer reports a suspected account compromise, the agent must advise changing the password, logging out of all devices (*Account → Security*), and escalate to the Trust & Safety team.
- Order details can be discussed only for orders on the logged-in customer's own account.

## S11 — Escalation & compensation
- Agents cannot issue goodwill compensation, extra discounts, or refunds outside this policy.
- If a customer is unhappy with a resolution, the agent can raise an escalation ticket; a senior agent responds within **24 hours**.
- Legal threats, harassment or safety concerns are escalated to the Trust & Safety team.

## S12 — Agent style guide
- Keep replies under **120 words**.
- Start with a short acknowledgement of the customer's issue (one sentence).
- Use plain sentences or a short bulleted list; **no markdown tables, no headings**.
- Use ₹ for amounts and "business days" for timelines exactly as in the policy.
- Be polite and calm; never blame the customer.
- **End with one clear next step** the customer should take.
- If something is not covered by the policy, say so and offer an escalation instead of guessing.
